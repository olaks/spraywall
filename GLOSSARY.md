# Spraywall

Setting and browsing boulder problems on a spray wall, using photos of the wall.

## Language

**Wall**:
A physical climbing wall, such as a home spray wall, that problems are set on.
_Avoid_: Board, panel

**Layout**:
A wall as it stands between two resets: one arrangement of holds. A wall has one current layout; earlier layouts are archived along with their photos and problems.
_Avoid_: Set, setup, version, configuration

**Wall photo**:
A picture of a layout. A layout can have several photos showing it from different angles or covering different parts of it.
_Avoid_: Image, picture, background

**Problem**:
A named boulder climb on a layout, defined by the holds it highlights. No two problems on the same layout share a name.
_Avoid_: Route, climb, boulder

**Mark**:
A highlight drawn on a wall photo to show that a hold belongs to one problem. Marks belong to their problem; holds are not recorded on their own.
_Avoid_: Highlight, hold (when you mean the drawn mark), annotation

**Overlapping marks**:
Marks from different problems on the same wall photo whose circles cross or touch. They usually mean the problems share a hold.
_Avoid_: Shared hold (holds are not recorded), same hold, collision

**Role**:
What a marked hold is for in its problem: start, hand, foot or finish.
_Avoid_: Type, hold type, colour

**Grade**:
How hard a problem is, given on its wall's grade scale. A problem may be ungraded while it is still a project.
_Avoid_: Difficulty, level

**Grade scale**:
The system a wall's problems are graded in: Font or V. Each wall has one; grades are never converted between scales.
_Avoid_: Grading system, grade type

**Retired problem**:
A problem taken out of use while its layout is still current, usually because one of its holds was moved. It is kept and can be viewed, like an archived layout.
_Avoid_: Deleted, broken, archived (that's for layouts)

**Backup**:
A file holding walls with their layouts, photos and problems, exported from the app and imported back into it. Importing adds what isn't already there; it never removes anything.
_Avoid_: Export file, archive (that's for layouts), snapshot

**Reset**:
Changing the holds on a wall so that its current layout no longer matches it. A reset starts a new layout; the old one is archived, not deleted.
_Avoid_: Rebuild, re-set
